# Sub-skill: Email Copy

Generates email sequences, individual sends, and templates across the funnel.
Always read `BRAND.md` in full before generating any content.

## When to use
- Onboarding sequences (welcome, activation, milestones)
- Nurture campaigns (educational, product tips)
- Announcements (launches, updates, events)
- Re-engagement (win-back, churn-risk)
- Transactional (receipts, confirmations, alerts — tone adjustment required)

## Voice constraints (from BRAND.md)
- Warm, conversational — not a press release
- First-person brand voice ("We built this because...") sparingly; lead with "you"
- Subject lines: curiosity or benefit, never clickbait
- One job per email — resist adding secondary CTAs
- Reply-friendly: end transactional emails with an invitation to respond

## Format templates

### Single email
```
Subject:    [≤50 characters | benefit or curiosity hook — no emoji unless BRAND.md approves]
Preview:    [≤90 characters | extends or contrasts the subject — not a repeat]

Opening:    [1 sentence — acknowledge where the reader is right now]
Body:       [2-4 short paragraphs OR bullets — 150-300 words total]
            [One idea per paragraph. Use plain transitions: "Here's why", "That means", "The result"]
CTA:        [One button or bolded link — action verb + specific outcome]
Sign-off:   [Name or team — match brand voice]
P.S.:       [Optional — restate CTA or add a single proof point. P.S. lines have high read rates.]
```

### Email sequence plan (before writing individual emails)
```
Sequence name: [e.g., New customer onboarding]
Goal:          [What behavioral change or milestone this sequence drives]
Trigger:       [What starts the sequence — signup, purchase, inactivity, etc.]

| # | Send timing | Subject angle | Goal | CTA |
|---|------------|--------------|------|-----|
| 1 | Immediately | Welcome + first win | Activate | [action] |
| 2 | Day 2       | [angle] | [goal] | [action] |
| 3 | Day 5       | [angle] | [goal] | [action] |
...
```

## Subject line formulas
| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Benefit + timeframe | "Set up [Feature] in 3 minutes" |
| Question the reader asks themselves | "Why isn't [X] working for you?" |
| Curiosity gap | "What most [audience] get wrong about [topic]" |
| Direct number | "3 things to do before [deadline/event]" |
| Social proof | "How [Customer] achieved [result] with [Product]" |

**Avoid:** ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation, "FREE", vague words like "Important" or "Check this out"

## Quality checks before output
- [ ] Subject ≤50 characters and passes the "would I open this?" test
- [ ] Preview text is not a repetition of the subject
- [ ] One primary CTA — no competing asks
- [ ] Word count 150-300 for nurture/promotional; transactional can be shorter
- [ ] Terminology matches BRAND.md table
- [ ] No unsubscribe-baiting language ("You can always unsubscribe if you hate value")
- [ ] Compliance footer placeholder included if required by BRAND.md
